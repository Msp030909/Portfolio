What is FRACTURED?


FRACTURED is a top down shooter that gives you a unique experience every time you play.
The game starts with you arriving in a small, far-away town. From there, you're free to explore.
But be careful; Even your smallest choices will completely change the story.
In one playthrough you'll be commiting heists, and in another, surviving a nuclear fallout.
Maybe you'll be a secret spy, or the CEO of a company.

Of course, it's not just visuals that change. The weapons you get, the people you fight, all depend on what you do.
Maybe as a spy you'd need to stealth through everything, but what's stopping you from going all out as part of the Special Forces?
Fighting police officers while robbing a bank is bound to different from fighting zombies during an apocalypse.
Sometimes you'll have to fend off with nothing but a pistol, while other times you'll be mowing down enemies left and right.
FRACTURED is by no means infinite, but it's got enough content to keep you entertained for a long, long time.

FRACTURED

Important thing to note: While FRACTURED is inspired by several roguelikes, it is not a roguelike.
Their biggest similarities will probably be found in the movement and gunplay.
The game has no procedural generation of any kind. All “routes” are made by hand.
